Focus list - what it means

The Focus list keeps customers that are not "Ok / monitor".

Expected counts (current build):
- 1,515 customers total on the focus list
- 1,267 High risk / high value
-   248 Elevated risk (top 20%) only

Rules
1) High risk / high value
   Churn_Prob >= 0.50 AND MonthlyCharges >= overall median (~70.35)

2) Elevated risk (top 20%)
   Churn_Prob is in the highest fifth of all customers
   (and not already tagged as high risk / high value)

Why these cutoffs
0.50 is the default classification threshold — easy to explain.
Median monthly spend separates "save first" revenue from lower-ARPU noise.
Top 20% mirrors the Medicare scorecard's bottom-quintile screening idea:
pull a manageable review queue without pretending the cutoff is sacred.

The full customers sheet still has every account, including "Ok / monitor".
That is normal. The Focus list page filters those out.
