Customer Churn Retention Scorecard - Scope

Question
Which customers are most likely to leave, which of those are worth saving
first, and what product / contract patterns drive churn?

Grain
One row = one customer (customerID)

Source
IBM Telco Customer Churn sample (public). Used for portfolio / interview
demonstration of a retention analytics workflow — not a live production
telecom database.

KPIs
- Churn rate (actual Yes/No)
- MonthlyCharges / Estimated annual revenue at risk
- Churn_Prob / Churn_Risk_Score (0-100) from logistic regression
- Segment churn rates: Contract, TechSupport, InternetService, PaymentMethod,
  Tenure_Band

Dashboard filters (suggested for Power BI)
Contract, TechSupport, InternetService, Tenure_Band, Focus_Flag, PaymentMethod

Model
Logistic regression on an 80/20 stratified train/test split (random_state=42).
Primary decision metrics: precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC (not accuracy alone).

Focus list
1) High risk / high value — Churn_Prob >= 0.50 and MonthlyCharges >= median
2) Elevated risk (top 20%) — Churn_Prob in the top fifth of all customers
